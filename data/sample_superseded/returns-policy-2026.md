# Acme Returns Policy (2026 edition)

## Standard return window

Customers may return any unopened item within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund. Opened items may be returned within 14 days provided all original
packaging is included.

## Restocking fee

The restocking fee has been abolished. No fee is deducted from refunds.

## Refund processing time

Refunds are issued within 5 business days of the returned item arriving at the
warehouse.

# Acme Returns Policy (2024 edition)

## Standard return window

Customers may return any unopened item within 14 days of delivery for a full
refund. Opened items are not eligible for return under this policy.

## Restocking fee

A restocking fee of 15 percent applies to all returned items. The fee is
deducted from the refund before it is issued.

## Refund processing time

Refunds are issued within 21 business days of the returned item arriving at
the warehouse.

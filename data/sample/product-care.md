# Acme Product Care and Maintenance

## Cleaning

Wipe hardware exteriors with a dry or slightly damp microfibre cloth only. Do not use
solvents, abrasive cleaners, or submerge any Acme device in water. Using a solvent or
abrasive cleaner on the housing voids the manufacturing-defect warranty for cosmetic and
housing-related claims.

## Storage

Store devices between 5C and 35C at under 80% humidity. For seasonal storage longer than
3 months, remove batteries from the device; batteries left installed during long storage
can leak and cause corrosion damage, which is treated as accidental damage and is not
covered by the warranty.

## Battery care

Consumable batteries perform best when not fully discharged before recharging. Battery
capacity naturally declines with use; the warranty covers batteries only against
capacity falling below 70 percent of rated value within 12 months, not gradual capacity
loss beyond that window.

## When maintenance voids warranty

Opening the housing, using third-party replacement parts, or attempting a
user-performed repair on a component not designated as user-replaceable voids the
remaining manufacturing-defect warranty on that unit.

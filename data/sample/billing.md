# Acme Billing and Payments

## Accepted payment methods

Acme accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express credit cards, as well as SEPA direct
debit for customers with an EU bank account. PayPal is accepted at checkout but not for
subscription renewals. Acme does not accept cryptocurrency or bank wire for orders under
500 EUR.

## Invoices

A VAT invoice is generated automatically at the time of shipment and emailed to the
address on the order. Business customers needing a pro-forma invoice before payment
should request one through support at least 2 business days before the order ships.
All prices displayed at checkout include VAT for EU deliveries.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, Acme retries automatically after 24 hours and again after 72
hours. After a third failed attempt the order is cancelled and the reserved stock is
released. SEPA direct debit failures are not retried automatically; the customer must
resubmit payment within 5 days or the order is cancelled.

## Subscriptions

Consumable subscriptions (filters, batteries) bill on the same calendar day each month
as the first order. A price change on a subscription takes effect no sooner than 30 days
after the customer is notified by email.

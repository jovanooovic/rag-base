# Acme Complaints and Escalation

## Standard support

Support tickets receive a first response within 1 business day and a resolution target
of 5 business days for most requests. Warranty and refund tickets follow the timelines
in their own policies rather than this general target.

## Escalating a complaint

If a ticket is not resolved within the target window, the customer can request
escalation to a senior support agent, who responds within 2 business days of the
escalation request. Escalations related to a refused warranty or refund claim are
reviewed by a different agent than the one who made the original decision.

## Formal complaints

If escalation does not resolve the issue, customers in the EU can file a formal
complaint through Acme's designated complaints address, which acknowledges receipt
within 3 business days and issues a final written response within 20 business days.
EU customers who remain unsatisfied after a final written response may refer the
complaint to their national consumer dispute resolution body.

## What escalation does not change

Escalating a complaint does not pause the standard return or warranty claim windows;
customers should still submit a return or warranty claim within the normal deadlines
while a complaint is under review.

# Acme Security and Data Privacy

## Data we retain

Acme retains order and payment metadata (not full card numbers) for 7 years to meet tax
record-keeping obligations, even after an account is deleted. Browsing and marketing
preference data is retained for 24 months of inactivity before automatic deletion.

## Your data rights

Customers in the EU and UK can request a copy of their personal data or request its
deletion under GDPR. Data export requests are fulfilled within 30 days. Deletion
requests are fulfilled within 30 days except for the tax records described above, which
are retained regardless of a deletion request until the 7-year period elapses.

## Encryption

Payment data is tokenised at checkout and Acme's systems never store full card numbers.
Data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher; data at rest in Acme's order
database is encrypted with AES-256.

## Breach notification

In the event of a data breach affecting customer information, Acme notifies affected
customers by email within 72 hours of confirming the breach, in line with GDPR
notification requirements.

# Acme Returns and Refunds Policy

## Standard return window

Customers may return any unopened item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Opened items may be returned within 14 days provided all original packaging is included.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of us receiving
the returned item.

## Non-returnable items

Perishable goods, custom-engraved products, and gift cards cannot be returned.
Clearance items marked FINAL SALE cannot be returned or exchanged.

## Return shipping

Acme covers return shipping when the item arrived damaged or was sent in error.
In all other cases the customer pays return shipping, which is deducted from the refund.
The current deduction is a flat 6.50 EUR per parcel.

## Order cancellation

An order can be cancelled at no cost until it enters the packing stage, usually within
two hours of being placed. After packing begins the order must be received and returned
under the standard return process.

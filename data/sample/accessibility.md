# Acme Accessibility

## Standards we target

The Acme storefront and account pages target WCAG 2.2 level AA. An external audit is
commissioned once a year and the summary report is published on the accessibility page.

## Screen readers and keyboard use

Every checkout step can be completed with a keyboard alone, and all form fields carry
programmatic labels. The storefront is tested against NVDA on Windows and VoiceOver on
macOS and iOS before each release.

## Reporting a barrier

Accessibility problems are reported through the standard support queue and are triaged
within two business days rather than the usual five. A workaround is offered in the
first reply where one exists.

## Alternative formats

Order confirmations and invoices can be issued as plain-text email instead of HTML on
request. The setting applies to all future orders on the account.

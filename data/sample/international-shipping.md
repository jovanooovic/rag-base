# Acme International Shipping

## Countries served

Acme ships to all EU member states, the UK, Switzerland, Norway, the United States, and
Canada. Acme does not currently ship to addresses outside these regions.

## Customs and duties

Shipments outside the EU are sent DDU (delivery duty unpaid): the customer is
responsible for any import duties, customs fees, or local taxes assessed on arrival.
Acme does not pre-pay these charges on any order, consumer or business. Estimated
duties are shown at checkout but are not guaranteed, since customs authorities set the
final assessment.

## Delivery times

UK and Switzerland: 4 to 7 business days. United States and Canada: 7 to 12 business
days. Norway: 5 to 8 business days. These are in addition to the 1 to 2 business days
of order processing before dispatch.

## Tracking

International shipments use the same ACM-XXXXXXX tracking code format as domestic EU
orders, with an additional customs-clearance status step that can pause tracking
updates for up to 3 business days without indicating a problem.

## Returns on international orders

International returns follow the standard return window, but the return-shipping
deduction is 18 EUR per parcel instead of the domestic 6.50 EUR, to cover the higher
cost of return freight. Acme does not refund import duties already paid by the customer
on a returned item.

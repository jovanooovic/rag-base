# Acme Press and Media

## Press enquiries

Press enquiries go to the media desk rather than the support queue. The desk replies
within one business day on weekdays, and does not handle individual order problems.

## Using the Acme name and logo

The Acme wordmark and logo may be used in articles and reviews without asking, provided
they are not altered, recoloured, or placed on a background that implies partnership.
Commercial use in advertising needs written permission from the media desk first.

## Product images

High-resolution product images are supplied on request for editorial use. Images may be
cropped but not otherwise edited, and must keep their original caption credit.

## Review units

Acme does not lend review units to individuals. Publications with a masthead can request
one through the media desk, and units are returned at Acme's cost.

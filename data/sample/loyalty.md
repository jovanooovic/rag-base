# Acme Rewards Loyalty Program

## Earning points

Members earn 1 point per 1 EUR spent, excluding shipping charges and gift card
purchases. Points post to the account 5 business days after the return window for the
order has passed, so a return within the standard window cancels the points earned on
the returned items.

## Tiers

Acme Rewards has three tiers, assigned automatically based on points earned in the
trailing 12 months:

- Bronze: 0-499 points. Standard benefits only.
- Silver: 500-1999 points. Free standard shipping on all orders.
- Gold: 2000+ points. Free express shipping, and the standard return window is extended
  from 30 days to 45 days on unopened items. The 14-day window for opened items is not
  extended.

## Redeeming points

Points redeem at a rate of 100 points = 5 EUR off a future order, in increments of 100.
Points cannot be redeemed for cash, combined with a gift card at checkout, or used to
pay for express delivery.

## Expiry

Points expire 24 months after they post if the account has no purchase activity in that
window. Tier status is reassessed every 1 January based on the trailing 12 months of
spend.

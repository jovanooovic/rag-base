# Acme Careers

## How hiring works

Open roles are listed on the Acme careers page. Every application gets a decision, and
the process runs to four stages: a screen, a take-home exercise, two interviews, and a
reference check. Candidates hear back within ten working days of each stage.

## Remote and hybrid roles

Roles are marked remote, hybrid, or on-site in the listing. Remote roles are open to
candidates in the EU and the UK; Acme does not sponsor visas for remote positions.

## Take-home exercises

Take-home exercises are capped at four hours and are never used in production work.
Candidates who reach that stage are paid for the time whether or not they are hired.

## Reapplying

Unsuccessful candidates can reapply for a different role immediately, or for the same
role after six months.

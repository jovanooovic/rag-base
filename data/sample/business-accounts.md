# Acme Business and Wholesale Accounts

## Eligibility and setup

A business account requires a valid VAT or business registration number. Approval
typically takes 2 business days. Business accounts are billed in the same currencies
and accept the same payment methods as consumer accounts, plus net-30 invoicing for
approved accounts with an order history of at least 3 consumer orders or 5,000 EUR in
prior spend.

## Pricing and minimums

Bulk pricing applies at 50+ units of a single SKU (10% off list price) and 200+ units
(18% off list price). There is no minimum order value for a business account, but
orders under 20 units are billed at standard consumer pricing.

## Account management

Business accounts spending more than 25,000 EUR per year are assigned a dedicated
account manager. Below that threshold, business accounts use the same support channels
as consumer accounts.

## Shipping and returns

Bulk business orders shipped internationally follow the customs and duties terms in
Acme's international shipping policy; Acme does not pre-pay import duties on business
orders regardless of destination. The standard return window applies per unit, but
returns of 50+ units require a support ticket to schedule a freight pickup rather than
the standard parcel return process.

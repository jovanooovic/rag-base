# Acme Account Management

## Password reset

A password reset link is sent to the account email and expires after 30 minutes. After
5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes as a brute-force
precaution.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) via SMS or an authenticator app can be enabled from
account settings. Business accounts with net-30 billing terms are required to enable
2FA.

## Changing your email

Changing the email on an account requires confirming the change from both the old and
the new address. The confirmation links expire after 24 hours. Orders already placed
under the old email are not affected.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion is processed within 30 days of the request. Order history required for
tax and warranty records is retained for 7 years even after account deletion, per Acme's
data retention policy, but is no longer linked to a login.

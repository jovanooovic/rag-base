# Acme Shipping Guide

## Delivery times

Standard delivery within the EU takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes
1 to 2 business days and costs an additional 12.00 EUR. Orders placed after 14:00 CET
are processed the following business day.

## Tracking

Every shipment receives a tracking code in the format ACM-XXXXXXX sent by email once the
parcel leaves the warehouse. Tracking updates can lag the physical parcel by up to 24 hours.

## Undeliverable parcels

If a parcel cannot be delivered after three attempts it returns to our warehouse in Rotterdam
and is automatically refunded minus the original shipping cost.

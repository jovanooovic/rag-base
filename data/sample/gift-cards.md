# Acme Gift Cards

## Purchasing

Gift cards are sold in fixed denominations of 25, 50, 100, and 200 EUR, delivered by
email within 1 hour of purchase. Physical gift cards are not available.

## Redeeming

A gift card code is entered at checkout and applies to the order total before shipping.
Multiple gift cards can be combined on a single order, but a gift card cannot be
combined with loyalty points at checkout.

## Balance and expiry

Gift card balances do not expire. Remaining balance after a partial redemption stays on
the same code and can be checked from the account page without needing to place an
order.

## Returns

Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot themselves be returned once purchased. If an
item bought with a gift card is returned, the refund is issued back to the same gift
card code rather than to a payment card.

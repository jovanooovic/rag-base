# Acme Warranty Terms

## Coverage

All Acme hardware carries a 24-month limited warranty from the date of delivery covering
manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid ingress,
or damage caused by third-party accessories.

## Claim process

Warranty claims start with a support ticket including the order number and a photograph of
the defect. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at Acme's discretion within 15 business
days. Warranty replacements do not restart the 24-month period.

## Batteries

Batteries are treated as consumables and are covered for 12 months only, and only against
capacity falling below 70 percent of the rated value.
